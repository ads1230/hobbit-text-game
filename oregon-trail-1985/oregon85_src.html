About

MECC's 1985 The Oregon Trail for the Apple II (version 1.4), ported program by program from the Applesoft BASIC on its two disks: the store, the day-by-day travel with its weather, health, illnesses and mishaps, the river crossings, trading, the people you can talk to at each landmark, the Barlow Road and the Columbia, and the Top Ten. Every price, probability and message is the original's; the hunting and rafting screens were rebuilt from the game's machine code, with the same animals, speeds and timing. The pictures, sprites and tunes are the disks' own, shown with the colour rules of an Apple II monitor. The numbered questions are buttons here, and the arcade bits take touch as well as keys.

Hunting: tap where you want to aim (or use the turn buttons; on a keyboard, arrows turn and I O P K ; , . / aim), Walk toggles walking, Fire shoots — one bullet at a time. Rafting: left and right.
