About

The Hobbit is © 1982 Melbourne House / Beam Software, written by Philip Mitchell and Veronika Megler. The built-in tape is the Sinclair Research Ltd re-release of 1982; any other version can be loaded above. The emulation follows the approach of Wilderland, using addresses from the SkoolKit disassembly, and the Z80 core is Molly Howell's Z80.js (MIT). Character set from the Spectrum ROM, © Amstrad, used with its permission for emulators.
