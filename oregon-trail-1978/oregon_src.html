About

A faithful port of the original text Oregon Trail, written in 1971 by Don Rawitsch, Bill Heinemann and Paul Dillenberger for the Minnesota Educational Computing Consortium, from the BASIC listing MECC published in Creative Computing in 1978. Every figure, formula and random mishap is the 1978 program's; only the way you answer has changed — the teletype's numbered questions are buttons here, and the trusty rifle still wants the word typed fast. Two slips in the printed listing (the day-of-week table and the month boundaries on arrival) are corrected.

The pictures, the map and the tombstone are from MECC's 1985 Apple II version, decoded from its disks. That game measures the trail by eighteen landmarks; they are placed here at the mileages of the 1978 game, with its two mountain passages (South Pass at 950 miles, the Blue Mountains at 1700) lining up with theirs.
