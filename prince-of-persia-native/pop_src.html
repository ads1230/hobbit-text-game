About

Jordan Mechner's Prince of Persia (Brøderbund, 1989), rebuilt in JavaScript from his published Apple II source: the same sequence tables, frame definitions, collision rules, guard programs, level blueprints and image tables, drawn with the same hi-res routines. Every frame has been checked byte for byte against the game running on an Apple IIe emulator. The music and sounds are recordings of the Apple's speaker.

The Macintosh graphics are the shapes of the 1992 Macintosh version (Brøderbund; programmed by Presage Software), drawn by this same Apple II game through the Mac version's own piece tables and placement rules, in its black-and-white (512 × 334) or 256-colour (640 × 400) art, with its title pages, its princess's room and its bottom band of meters and messages in its own typeface.
