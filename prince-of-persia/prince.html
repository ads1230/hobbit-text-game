About

Jordan Mechner's Prince of Persia (Brøderbund, 1989), from the three Apple II disk sides. The page is a small Apple IIe: a 65C02, 128K, the language card and a Disk II in slot 6, with the game's own loader doing the rest. There is no Apple ROM in it — the handful of monitor routines the boot uses are re-implemented. Disk sides are swapped for you when the game asks.
